## Decisions Made

- **R-2 path a (PositionViewer single-mount):** PositionViewer renders only in the Vault view. The freed Risk `r2` left cell is filled by pulling `EventStreamPanel` up into it (Risk grid collapses to 2 rows: r1 surface+side, r2 event-stream | what-if, r3 lone backtest card). This avoids two live `position-viewer` anchors (D-1) and leaves no empty cell.
- **Mint active-state color-mix:** `color-mix(in oklab, var(--accent) 12%, var(--surface))` — the exact value used on `.db-mode-trigger[data-state='active']`. Same accent reservation family as the active rail item (`color-mix(accent 10–12%)`); coral (`--hedge`) deliberately absent.
- **`event-stream` is now the Risk r2 left anchor** — a stable, mounted target for Plan 04.2-02's observer reconciliation (R-4). In Risk mode the mounted `[data-section]` anchors are `hero`, `exposure`, `event-stream`, `what-if`, `backtest`; in Vault mode they are `deposit-withdraw` and `position-viewer`.
